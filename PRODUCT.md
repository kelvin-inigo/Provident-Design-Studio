# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Provident Real Estate's in-house designers, who make the company's Meta ads and organic social posts every day, on Mac desktops in Chrome. Around them: the content team (who check the words) and performance marketing (who file campaigns as tickets and run the finished ads).

## Product Purpose

Provident Design Studio is two browser studios on one brand system: the Campaign Ads Studio (CAS) for paid Meta ads in every size, and the Organic Post Studio (OPS) for locked-template feed and story posts. Success is an on-brand ad or post exported in minutes, with no off-grid layouts and nothing the brand rules forbid.

## Positioning

The layouts are Provident's own: every component, template, margin and type step is measured off the brand's drawings and rules, and the editor draws exactly what the export ships.

## Operating Context

- A campaign starts as a ticket from performance marketing, is designed per audience and language (English, then Arabic), goes to the content team's copy check, and ends as a package performance marketing downloads.
- Work is saved as session files in a Source folder; each studio remembers recent saved projects on the computer.
- The site's landing page (index.html, provident-design-studio.vercel.app) is the door to both studios.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single-file HTML apps, no build step; they open from the disk or the Vercel site.
- Google Sans Flex is the only typeface (Readex Pro for Arabic).
- The chrome follows `ui-design-system/`; canvas artwork follows the brand palettes in code, never chrome tokens.

## Brand Commitments

- The "provident." wordmark (with the Design Studio lockup in the chrome); orange #F3793C is punctuation, never a fill on dark.
- Provident Navy #1A2942 and its tint ramp; no gold in the chrome.
- Plain, direct copy; sentence case in the chrome.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real template renders in `preview/`.
- Hairline line figures (vendor/hairline): Elevator for Campaign ads, Turntable for Organic posts.
- No testimonials, metrics or customer claims exist; none may be invented.

## Product Principles

- What the editor shows is what ships.
- The brand rules are enforced by the tool, not remembered by the designer.
- Getting back to the work in hand beats browsing options.
