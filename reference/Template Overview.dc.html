Template overview
Reference sheet for designers. Every template exports at 1080 × 1440 (feed 3:4) and 1080 × 1920 (story 9:16) with the same margin grid — 80px sides, 90px top and bottom on feed; 100px sides and bottom with a 200px top clearance on 9:16. Scrims never cover the whole photo: they band the text areas with 20px breathing room and a long eased fade, and one slider controls every slide of a post. Serif accents are always upright, never italic. Layouts are fixed; only the fields change. Production happens in the Organic Post Studio.
