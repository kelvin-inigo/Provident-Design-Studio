Provident ad design system

The 12 modules recurring across 32 campaign ads, rebuilt to the 2026 brand guideline: Google Sans Flex only (300/400/500), Source Serif 4 reserved for single-word emphasis in the biggest headline only (upright, never italic), orange on light backgrounds only, gold on navy, flat brand surfaces. All type and spacing in container units so every canvas scales without breaking.

Two voices: Google Sans Flex carries everything. Headers are Light 300 — except the main hero headline (the biggest headline of the layout), which is Thin 100. Source Serif 4 — upright, never italic, always Gold #C9A96A — appears only as one emphasised word inside that hero headline; smaller headings, subheads and body never use it. It swaps the whole word only — never a syllable blended into a sans word. Meta floor: no type below 2.2cqw (≈24px at 1080) anywhere on an ad. Every size sits on the minor-third scale (×1.200 from the 2.2 floor): 2.2 · 2.6 · 3.2 · 3.8 · 4.6 · 5.5 · 6.6 · 7.9 · 9.5 — no off-scale sizes.

05 · Swap guide

New project, same system

Change freely
Backdrop — property render or photograph, always under the navy scrim
Colourway — imagery, Deep Navy, Provident Navy, or Cream
Headline copy, kicker copy, feature-strip benefits
Currency and figure in the price block — AED / $ / € swap in place
CTA label, QR destination, event dates and venue
Never moves
4% safe margin — nothing crosses it; 9:16 adds the Instagram safe zones
Image outweighs text — modules cover at most ~40% of the canvas; only essential elements survive: headline, one hook or spec, CTA, logo, QR
Logo left or centre, never right; QR a small rounded corner placeholder, never dominant
Two weights per lockup (300 setup / 400 payoff); figures 500, the word after them 300
Google Sans Flex everywhere; serif only as one emphasised word in the biggest headline — upright, never italic
No orange on navy — gold carries dark-surface accents; CTAs navy or white, never orange
