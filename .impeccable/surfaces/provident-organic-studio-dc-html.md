# OPS home (the Organic Post Studio's first screen)

Mode: Operate. Users: social media manager (drafts), designer (refines), content team (checks copy), daily, Mac + Chrome. Success: the post that is waiting on you is one click from its next step; a new post is named, briefed and on a template in three short steps. Constraints: OPS chrome (ui-design-system tokens, Google Sans Flex, navy ramp), posts on Google Drive (post-flow.js), PM sees Status only. Untouched: sidebar, Status board, recents data, templates' real renders.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Home is two doors behind one switch — the posts you owe, or a new post — refusing the dashboard that stacks a form, a gallery and a list on one page.
OWN-WORLD: OPS chrome as it stands: navy resting tint for the chosen half, ink-2 quiet text, 16px cards on --ps-card with hairline rings, the posts' real 3:4 renders as the only imagery, step segments in good/link/warn.
STORY: The person sees who they are and how many posts wait on them, acts on the top one without opening it, or switches to start a post: name → brief → template.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Greeting and count, then the My turn | Start a post switch under them, left — it reads in order after the greeting and keeps its place on a phone (amended after review), Drive status and Refresh at the switch row's right; below, full-width rows: render 72px, name, step + due, one primary action at the row's end; quieter "Everyone else's" rows; recent renders strip at the foot.
FORM: Two doors, candidate 7 of 7, seed eeaa4efa.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
