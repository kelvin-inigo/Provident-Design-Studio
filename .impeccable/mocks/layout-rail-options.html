Layout rail — three new directions

The canvas map is gone in all three. Each one answers the same four questions (where the copy sits, its alignment, the logo's corner, the canvas look) in a different place.

THE ROLL · A

Words, previewed on the real canvas

The rail is a plain list of choices in words. Point at one and the actual canvas shows it, outlined, before you click. No drawing in the rail to decode.

Why: you judge a layout by seeing the ad, not a diagram of it.
  • Hover = live preview, click = keep, Esc = back.
  • Takes the least room; the canvas does the showing.
  • Risk: nothing visual in the rail at rest; relies on hovering.

One layout field, keyboard-fast

The whole layout is one named choice: type or pick “Bottom · center · logo top left”. Every combination has a key, and the ones you use most sit as chips.

  • Risk: less visual; new designers must read the names.
